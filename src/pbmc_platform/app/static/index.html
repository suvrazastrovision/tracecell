Inspect a single-cell sample, choose your quality rules and keep the evidence behind every result.

Advanced settings and sample details

Detected from the file: available count matrices, their cell/gene counts, and gene annotation columns. Choose the correct original counts and gene columns; these numbers are read automatically and cannot be edited here.

For your own upload, enter or confirm species, mitochondrial mapping, QC thresholds, tissue, reference genome, source and licence. Any existing example settings are starting values, not facts inferred from your file.

5 / Compare settings on the same sample
Compare two completed QC runs to see which cells each set of settings keeps. This compares technical QC decisions, not independent biological evidence.
How to create a comparison
  1. Upload or choose a sample once and complete its first quality check.
  2. Select that run below, then click Prepare a second run. Its original sample and settings will load above.
  3. Change only the quality thresholds, such as maximum mitochondrial percentage from 5 to 10. Keep the species, counts and gene mappings unchanged. Do not upload the file again.
  4. Review, confirm and click Run quality check. Wait for it to finish.
  5. Select the two runs below and click Compare these runs. The second list only shows compatible runs of the same uploaded sample.
